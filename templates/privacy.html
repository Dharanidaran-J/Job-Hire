ChallengeHire Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

ChallengeHire collects candidate profile information (name, email, university, degree, and skills), recruiter profiles (name, official email, company affiliation), code repository URLs, live demo links, and challenge evaluation rubrics.

2. How We Use Data

Candidate skills and challenge submissions are used solely for job matching, practical scoring, and hiring team reviews. We do not sell user data to third-party data brokers.

3. AI Analysis & Explainability

AI algorithms deployed on ChallengeHire provide assistive rubric recommendations and skill gap roadmaps. Recruiters make final hiring decisions following holistic candidate reviews.
